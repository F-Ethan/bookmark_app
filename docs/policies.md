# Bookmark: Horner Bible Reading — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

_Last updated: [fill in publish date]_

## Overview

Bookmark is a Bible reading tracker built around Horner's Bible Reading System. This page
covers both our Privacy Policy (what data we collect and why) and our Terms of Service (the
rules for using the app), since they're short enough to live together.

## Using the app without an account (Guest Mode)

You can use Bookmark entirely without creating an account. In this mode, everything — your
reading plan, progress, saved verses — stays only on your device. We never see it, and it's
lost if you uninstall the app or switch devices. Nothing you do in Guest Mode contributes to
or is visible in the community features described below.

## What we collect if you create an account

If you create an account, we collect and store:

- **Your email address and password**, used only to sign you in (handled by our backend
  provider, Supabase; we never see your password in plain text).
- **Your reading plan**: the name you enter, your current reading day, your book-group layout,
  and your reading streak / highest day reached.
- **Verses you save** ("highlights"): the verse text, reference, and the day you saved it.
  These are private to you — no other user can see them.
- **An anonymized count of how many people highlighted a given verse** may be shown to other
  users as "N people also highlighted this" — this never reveals who highlighted it, only a
  number.

We do not collect or store any payment information — see "Supporters," below.

## The Leaderboard (opt-in)

The Leaderboard is entirely optional. If you turn it on in Settings:

- You choose a **display name** — this can be different from the name you use elsewhere in the
  app, so you don't have to share your real name if you don't want to.
- Your display name and two stats (your longest reading streak, and the highest day you've
  reached) become visible to other users on the Leaderboard screen.
- We never show your email, your account name, or anything you've read or highlighted — only
  the display name and stats you've explicitly opted to share.
- You can turn this off at any time in Settings, which removes you from the Leaderboard
  immediately.
- **Display names should be appropriate for a general audience.** We may remove a display name
  (and, if needed, opt you out of the Leaderboard) if it's offensive, impersonates someone else,
  or is otherwise inappropriate.

## Supporters

Bookmark is a personal project, built and maintained by one developer (Ethan) — thank you for
using it. If you'd like to support that work, reach out at **ethanferrier@gamelogic.dev** and
you'll be added to the in-app Supporters list by hand (your name, and optionally a logo and a
link if you're a business). If we set up a Patreon page in the future, that will be handled
entirely by Patreon — we'll never see or store your payment details ourselves. Being listed as a
supporter never unlocks any feature or removes any limitation — everything in Bookmark remains
free either way.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings. This is
permanent and cannot be undone.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove a display name or supporter listing? Email
us at support@gamelogic.dev.
